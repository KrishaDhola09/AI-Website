Discover Artificial Intelligence

AI is changing the way we learn, work and solve problems.

What is AI?

Artificial Intelligence is a technology that helps computers learn, solve problems and perform tasks that usually require human intelligence.

Benefits of AI

AI saves time, supports education and helps people analyze information.

Drawbacks of AI

AI can make mistakes, create privacy risks and lead to overdependence on technology.

Case Study: AI in Education

Problem

Students may find difficult topics hard to understand.

AI Solution

An AI learning assistant can explain topics simply and provide practice questions.

Result

Students can practise at their own pace while teachers guide their learning.
